Frequently asked questions

What is Kolmos?

An app that turns Israeli news sites into a daily Hebrew newspaper: one article per event, organized into sections, on pages you turn like a printed paper.

When does a new edition come out?

Twice a day: a morning edition at about 06:00 and an evening edition at about 18:00, Israel time. If a new edition arrives while you’re reading, Kolmos tells you and you can switch to it.

Where does the news come from?

From the public RSS feeds of ynet, Walla, Israel Hayom, Maariv, Globes, Davar, Zman Yisrael, Srugim, Kikar HaShabbat, Arutz 7 and Geektime. The mix is deliberate, so most events are covered from more than one point of view.

Who writes the articles?

An AI model. It reads every outlet’s report on the same event and writes a short article in its own words. It is instructed to use only facts that appear in the sources and to say when they disagree. Mistakes are still possible: every article links to its sources, so check them before relying on an important detail.

I found a mistake in an article.

Email avitsadok@me.com with the edition date (morning or evening), the page number and the headline. It helps us improve the writing.

Can I read offline?

Yes. Each edition is saved on your device when it downloads, so you can read it on a plane or a train. Links to the original stories need a connection.

How do I change the font, paper, dark mode or text size?

Tap תצוגה (Display) in the bottom bar. Choose a layout, a paper, light or dark mode (or by time of day), and a text size. The preview updates as you choose.

How do I jump to a section?

The front page has an index, בגיליון היום (In today’s paper), with each section’s page number. You can also tap עמודים (Pages) in the bottom bar.

The edition won’t load.

Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the app. Offline, Kolmos shows the last edition it downloaded. If it keeps happening, email us.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Kolmos is free, with no ads, no subscription and no sign-up.

Which devices does Kolmos run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac. On iPad and Mac the page is wider and shows more columns.

I work for an outlet that appears in Kolmos.

We’d like to hear from you. To ask for a correction, a change in credit or removal, email avitsadok@me.com and we’ll handle it quickly.

What does Kolmos know about me?

Very little: there’s no account and we never ask for personal details. The app sends anonymous usage statistics. The details are in the privacy policy.
