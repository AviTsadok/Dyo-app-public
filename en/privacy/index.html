Privacy policy

Your privacy in Dyo

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short

  • No sign-up and no account. Dyo never asks for personal details.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps and websites, and we don’t sell data to anyone.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics), so we know which sections and pages are read and what to improve.

Who we are

Dyo is an independent, non-commercial app developed by Avi Tsadok. “We” in this policy means Dyo’s developer. For any privacy question, email avitsadok@me.com.

What stays on your device

  • Downloaded editions (up to the latest 40), so you can read without a connection.
  • Your display settings: layout, paper, appearance and text size.

This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What Dyo’s server receives

To download an edition, the app contacts Dyo’s server, which runs on Cloudflare. Like any request on the internet, the server receives technical details: your IP address, the time, which edition was requested, and general OS version details. We use them only to deliver the edition and keep the service working and secure. Server logs are kept briefly and deleted automatically, and we don’t use them to identify you. Cloudflare processes this information as our service provider.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the paper is read: which sections are read, whether “continued on page” works, and which display settings people like. This is what’s sent:

  • Reading actions: opening an edition, moving between pages (page number and section), tapping a front-page story or a source link (the outlet’s name and website), how long the in-app browser stayed open, opening Pages and Display, and errors loading an edition.
  • Your display settings.
  • What Google collects automatically: a random identifier for this installation (not tied to your name, your Apple Account or the advertising identifier), device model, OS and app versions, language, and approximate country and city derived from your IP address.

Never sent: your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), or anything you type.

All of Google’s advertising options are turned off for Dyo: ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization. Google processes this data as our service provider; it is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted. See Google’s privacy policy.

Links to original stories

Every Dyo article links to its sources. On iPhone and iPad they open inside the app in Apple’s built-in Safari browser; on Mac, in your default browser. We can’t see what happens on those sites, and their own privacy policies apply. We only count that a link was opened and how long the window stayed open.

What we don’t do

  • Sell, rent or share data for advertising.
  • Track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Build a profile of you or link this data to your identity.

Children

Dyo is a general news app and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device, including the installation identifier. Because we don’t know who you are, we hold no data linked to your name. If you still want to access data, ask for deletion, or raise any privacy matter under Israel’s Privacy Protection Law or any other law that applies to you (such as the GDPR), email avitsadok@me.com and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect changes, for example when we add a new-edition notification, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Notifications will only be sent if you allow them on your device.
