Israel’s news, in one newspaper

Kolmos (Hebrew for “quill”) gathers stories from Israeli news sites, writes one article per event, and lays them out as the pages of a printed Hebrew newspaper. A morning and an evening edition, no endless feed.

Coming soon to the App Store

One event, one article

When five sites report the same event, Kolmos merges them into one short article and notes what each source said.

Looks like a newspaper

A front page, sections that start on a new page, columns and headlines. You turn pages instead of scrolling. Four layouts, five papers, dark mode.

Every source, linked

Each article ends with the outlets it was built from, linking to the original story. No ads, no sign-up.

The app itself is in Hebrew.
